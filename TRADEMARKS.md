# Trademarks

The code here is open: use it, change it and sell it under MIT or Apache 2.0. The name and the marks
are not part of that.

**What is protected:** the Streams name, the play mark, the wordmark and everything in `brand/streams`.

**What you may do:** run an unchanged copy, say truthfully that your product is built on this project,
and link to it.

**What you need to do for your own product:** add your own brand folder, set it in your config, and
use your own name and marks. One folder and one file change it everywhere.

**What you may not do:** call your product Streams, use the marks to identify it, or suggest that the
original project made or supports it.

Ask if you are unsure.
